Parents Weekend

Parents Weekend is October 23–26, 2025.

Parents Weekend Schedule

  • Thursday (Optional)
  • Friday
  • Saturday
  • Sunday

Thursday (Optional)

11:30 a.m.–5:00 p.m. Registration—Schoolhouse, Sackett Forum
Families pick up registration packets, schedules, and nametags and have an opportunity to meet fellow parents. Refreshments available all day.

1:00–5:00 p.m. Parent/Teacher Conferences—Schoolhouse
Parents meet with teachers in ten-minute intervals, as scheduled. Please see your specific schedule for details, times, and exact locations.

Friday

11:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m. Lunch—Dining Hall

11:30 a.m.–1:15 p.m. International Parents Luncheon—Headmaster’s House
A luncheon for our international families

11:30 a.m.–5:00 p.m. Registration—Schoolhouse, Sackett Forum
Families pick up registration packets, schedules, and nametags and have an opportunity to meet fellow parents. Refreshments available all day.

12:30–5:00 p.m. Parent/Teacher Conferences—Schoolhouse
Parents meet with teachers in ten-minute intervals, as scheduled. Please see your specific schedule for details, times, and exact locations.

4:00–5:00 p.m. Academic Skills and Counseling Open House—Schoolhouse, Ground Floor
Join us for an opportunity to meet the faculty from our support service departments, Counseling and Academic Skills.

4:45–5:05 p.m. Fifth Form College Counseling Information Session—Schoolhouse, Multipurpose Room
This is a meeting for parents of Fifth Form students to learn more about the counseling process and to hear from Peter Newcomb, Director of College Counseling.

5:00–7:30 p.m. Dinner—Dining Hall

5:10–5:30 p.m. Introduction to College Counseling—Schoolhouse, Multipurpose Room
This is an optional meeting for parents of Third and Fourth Form students to learn more about the counseling process and to hear from Peter Newcomb, Director of College Counseling.

5:30–6:00 p.m. Spiritual Life Open House—Schoolhouse, Room 140
Join Spiritual Life team leaders for refreshments and informal presentation and discussion of weekday Chapel services and our weekend religious gatherings.

6:00 p.m. Game Time Tent – Friday Night Lights—Athletic Center Terrace
Go Groton! Join parents and students under the tent for tailgate-themed food, and cheer for our Groton Zebras! 

6:30 p.m. Varsity Football vs. St. George's—Football Field
Details may be found on the website athletic calendar.

Saturday

7:00–9:00 a.m. Breakfast—Dining Hall
    
7:30–11:30 a.m. Registration—Schoolhouse, Sackett Forum
Families pick up registration packets, schedules, and nametags and have an opportunity to meet fellow parents. Refreshments available all day.
 

8:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. Parent/Teacher Conferences—Schoolhouse
Parents meet with teachers in ten-minute intervals, as scheduled. Please see your specific schedule for details, times, and exact locations.

10:30 a.m.–12:00 p.m. Parent Fund Volunteer Meeting—Schoolhouse, Multipurpose Room
Parent Fund volunteers and parents interested in learning more about Groton School’s Parent Fund are welcome to attend. The presentation begins at 11:00 a.m.

11:30 a.m.–1:30 p.m. Lunch—Dining Hall


1:30–2:15 p.m. Headmaster’s Address—Schoolhouse, Sackett Forum
Please join us to hear Headmaster Temba Maqubela’s Parents Weekend address.


2:15 p.m.–3:00 p.m. Global Education Opportunities (GEOs)—Schoolhouse, Room 127/128
Join Dean of Globalism and Experiential Learning, Nishad Das, and other members of Groton's faculty to learn more about Groton's Global Education Opportunities (GEOs). Our GEOs help students understand cultural, socioeconomic, and political disparities throughout the world.

2:15 p.m.–4:00 p.m. Open Rehearsal—Campbell Performing Arts Center, McBaine Studio Theater

3:00 p.m. Athletic Contests—Athletic Fields
Details may be found on the website athletic calendar.
    
5:00–7:00 p.m. Parent Reception—Sunset Farm, 154 Farmers Row, Groton
Stop by to enjoy the company of other parents at Sunset Farm. This reception is for adults only, as alcohol will be served. Continuous shuttles will depart from the Marion D. Campbell Performing Arts Center. You may also drive to Sunset Farm (one mile from campus), but parking is limited.

5:00–7:30 p.m. Dinner—Dining Hall

8:00–9:30 p.m. Parents Weekend Performance—Marion D. Campbell Performing Arts Center
Families welcome. Students who are performing should arrive at 7:00 p.m.

Sunday

10:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m. Brunch—Dining Hall
    
11:00 a.m. Chapel Service—St. John’s Chapel
    Episcopal service of Holy Communion with music from the Choir.

11:00 a.m. Buddhist Sangha—Hundred House, Memorial Room

12:15 p.m. Catholic Mass—St. John’s Chapel

Conference scheduling will open in early October, exact date to be announced.
