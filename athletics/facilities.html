Athletic Facilities

The hallmark of a Groton education is the development of the whole person—body, mind, and spirit—and superior athletic facilities play a role in this process.
Although our focus has always been on developing people, not facilities, the school believes that the right athletic environment can help our student athletes achieve their goals.
 

Outdoor Facilities

Seven athletic fields—including a lighted artificial turf field and a lighted natural grass field—for:

  • Football
  • Soccer
  • Field Hockey
  • Baseball
  • Lacrosse

A boathouse serving the crew program

10 outdoor tennis courts

A four-lane, 25-yard outdoor pool
 

Maqubela Track and Field Complex

The Maqubela Track and Field Complex,
completed in 2024 includes:

  • An eight lane 400m track
  • 2 jumping pits
  • High jump and pole vault landing pads
  • Discus cage
  • Shot put circle

 

Athletic Center

Interior athletic space for:

  • 12 international squash courts
  • 8 indoor tennis courts
  • 3 indoor basketball courts
  • 2 ice rinks
  • renovated and expanded locker rooms
  • weight and training rooms
  • an elevated indoor track
  • a multi-purpose/dance studio
  • a four-lane, 25-yard pool

Size: more than 250,000 square feet

Groton School Mission:

To inspire lives of character, scholarship, leadership, and service  within a diverse, inclusive, and close-knit community.
