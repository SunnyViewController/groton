Working at Groton

HA_09671.jpg

Working at Groton School means entering a close community that is dedicated to nurturing students in grades eight through 12 and preparing them, as the school’s founder Rev. Endicott Peabody said, for “the active work of life.” Groton School provides competitive salaries and an appealing benefit package to a diverse workforce of faculty and staff.

Faculty Positions

  • Director of Alumni Engagement
  • Assistant Director of Admission

Director of Alumni Engagement

Groton School seeks a dynamic and strategic Director of Alumni Engagement to lead efforts that foster lifelong relationships between the school and its global alumni community. Reporting to the Director of Development and Alumni Affairs and working closely with colleagues within the Alumni Office and across the school, the Director of Alumni Engagement is responsible for designing and implementing a comprehensive alumni engagement program that strengthens alumni connection, participation, and pride in Groton.

A successful candidate will bring creativity, strong relationship-building skills, and a deep appreciation for the mission and values of a residential independent school.

Non-teaching faculty members are provided with meals in the school Dining Hall, medical and dental insurance insurance, and retirement contributions. The starting salary range is $110,000-$130,000.

Please note that, prior to hire, the selected candidate will be asked to submit proof of vaccination against COVID-19.

Founded in 1884, Groton School is devoted to inspiring lives of character, scholarship, leadership, and service within a diverse, inclusive, and close-knit community. Three hundred eighty students in grades eight through twelve benefit from the guidance of more than ninety faculty members who live on a bucolic New England campus with world-class facilities, less than an hour’s drive from Boston, Massachusetts.

Groton School is committed to the principle of equal employment opportunity and seeks employees who reflect and support our mission-driven commitment to a diverse school community. The school complies with all government regulations regarding non-discriminating employment practices and, beyond that, provides an atmosphere that ensures all have the opportunity to succeed on their own merit, qualifications, and abilities. Groton School does not discriminate in employment opportunities or practices on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, sexual orientation, or any characteristics protected by law. This policy governs all aspects of employment, including selection, job assignment, compensation, discipline, termination, and access to benefits and training.

Interested candidates should send a resume and a letter of interest to Groton’s Human Resources at jobs@groton.org.

Groton School is an equal opportunity employer.

Assistant Director of Admission

Groton School seeks a person with the attributes, skills and character to draw talented students to one of the top secondary schools in the country.  Knowledge of candidate recruitment and assessment, publication development, presentation skills, and all other elements of admission work are helpful. It is important to be a motivated individual, a team player, and be committed to the growth and welfare of people in their adolescent years.

This is a faculty position beginning in July 2026. All faculty members at Groton are expected to contribute to the extra-curricular life of the school and share some dormitory and residential school responsibilities.  Extra-curricular contributions are particularly valuable in this position.
 
Faculty members are also expected to contribute to the extracurricular life of the school and share some dormitory and residential school responsibilities. Qualifications, therefore, include a demonstrated interest in coaching or coordinating extracurricular activities and a willingness to fulfill evening and weekend duties.
 
Faculty members are provided with living quarters, meals in the school Dining Hall, utilities (except for telephone and internet service), and medical insurance. The starting salary range is $60,000-$65,000.

Please note that, prior to hire, the selected candidate will be asked to submit proof of vaccination against COVID-19.

Teaching Fellow Program

  • Charles C. Alexander Teaching Fellow Program

Charles C. Alexander Teaching Fellow Program

Groton School, a coeducational boarding school in Groton, Massachusetts, welcomes applicants to the Charles C. Alexander Teaching Fellow Program. This program serves recent college graduates interested in working under supervision to develop the varied skills required of secondary boarding school teachers. The program offers as wide an exposure as possible to all aspects of boarding school life.

The teaching fellow's responsibilities will include teaching two classes under the supervision of an experienced member of the department as well as observation of other classes. The fields open to fellows are English, History, Mathematics, Classics, French, Spanish, Chinese, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Religion, and the Arts (not Art History), but the departments with openings vary from year to year. Openings will be listed on this page. Fellows also help coach athletics during at least two but preferably three seasons, assist in dormitory supervision one or two evenings per week and every fourth Saturday evening (or as needed), and participate in other areas of school life commensurate with their interests and talents.

Teaching fellow appointments are one-year, salaried positions. Also provided are living quarters, meals in the school's Dining Hall, and full utilities, except for telephone. Medical insurance is offered, with recipients paying a small percentage of the premium for the plan chosen.

We are accepting applications through January 10, 2027 for the 2027–28 school year.

College seniors and recent graduates interested in applying for the Fellows program should submit a cover letter; a resume indicating education, previous employment, interests, and athletic experience; a copy of their college transcript; and three letters of recommendation to the Charles C. Alexander Teaching Fellow Program, Groton School, 282 Farmers Row, Groton, MA 01450. Questions should be directed to Ms. Rita Lalli at 978-448-7502 or fellows@groton.org.

Staff Positions

  • Per Diem Nurse
  • Part-Time Medical Chaperone
  • Full-Time Campus Safety Officer
  • Assistant Athletic Trainer
  • Part-Time School Store and Mailroom Associate

Per Diem Nurse

Groton School is seeking registered nurse to work in the school’s Health Center on a per diem (as-needed) basis, beginning in June. 

Shifts are in 12-hour blocks and may vary between days, nights and weekends. 

The ideal candidates will have experience working with adolescents in an office, urgent care, emergency room, school setting, or have other relevant nursing experience.

Job responsibilities include:

  • first aid assessment

  • prescribed treatment of illness and injury

  • medication administration

  • triage

  • emergency response

  • electronic medical documentation in a school setting. 

Candidates should have a bachelor's in nursing with current Massachusetts RN license, a minimum of three years of nursing experience, and excellent communication skills.

To apply, please send a cover letter and resume to jobs@groton.org and reference Per Diem RN in the subject line.

Part-Time Medical Chaperone

Groton School is seeking part-time medical chaperones to transport students to off-campus appointments.

These are on-call positions during the Groton School academic year (early September through June).  

Responsibilities include:

  • Driving students to and from off-campus medical and behavioral health visits (in areas including, but not limited to, Boston and Manchester, NH).

Requirements:

  • A valid driver’s license and a good driving record
  • A clean and reliable passenger vehicle 
  • Evidence of adequate liability and collision automobile insurance
  • Ability to maintain confidentiality
  • Knowledge of adolescent issues and basic familiarity with the health care environment are helpful.

Please note that, prior to hire, this position will require a background check.

Please send your resume to jobs@groton.org with Medical Chaperone in the subject line.

Groton School is an equal opportunity employer.

Full-Time Campus Safety Officer

Groton School’s Buildings and Grounds Department is seeking a full-time Safety Officer to work Friday, Saturday and Sunday 3:00 p.m.–11:30 p.m. Monday and Tuesday 10:00 a.m.–6:30 p.m.

This is a 12-month-per-year, hourly position with the benefits afforded to all full-time staff members (including access to health, dental, and vision insurance and the school’s retirement savings program). 

This role reports to the Deputy Director of Buildings and Grounds.

Applicants must possess:

  • a valid Massachusetts driver’s license
  • CPR training (or a willingness to be trained upon hire)
  • the ability to pass a physical ability test and undergo a full background check
  • a positive attitude and strong interpersonal skills
  • the ability to (occasionally) shovel snow and lift heavy objects when needed
  • one to three years of experience in school or campus safety, law enforcement, military police training, or correctional officer training
  • the availability to cover other shifts as needed

Compensation

The starting hourly rate for this position is $23.00.

Please send your resume to jobs@groton.org, with Safety Officer in the subject line.

Groton School is an equal opportunity employer.
 

Assistant Athletic Trainer

Groton School is seeking an Assistant Athletic Trainer to provide evaluation, treatment, and rehabilitation for student injuries and immediate emergency care during athletic practices and competitions.

The Assistant Athletic Trainer will report to and work closely with the Director of Athletics and Head Athletics Trainer in the care of student athletes and in the administration of departmental policies, procedures, and guidelines.

Groton School is a coeducational boarding school offering athletic competition at various levels through forty-four athletic teams. The Assistant Athletic Trainer's typical work-week will be 35–40 hours, including some travel to away games.

Other responsibilities for this staff position will include, but are not limited to:

  • Working toward the prevention of and providing care for athletic injuries, along with rehabilitation duties
  • Collaborating with the Head Athletic Trainer in planning, organizing, directing, and executing programs associated with injury prevention
  • Providing education and serving as a resource for the Groton community on injury/illness prevention and wellness
  • Ensuring coverage of home practices and competitions (both Groton and neutral site
  • contests), including pre- and post-season coverage, as well as early vacation returns
  • Accurate record-keeping
  • Preparing both end-of-season and annual injury reports
  • Helping with medical supplies/equipment inventory
  • Administering and auditing concussion certifications
  • Providing respectful communication, within limits of confidentiality, on students' health matters to coaches, parents, health care providers, and school administrators, as needed
  • Maintaining complete and accurate records of athletic training services provided and pertinent medical data
  • Helping to maintain the athletic training room
  • Collaborating with the school's Wellness Program Manager as needed

Requirements:

  • Bachelor's degree in sports medicine or related field
  • NATA-BOC certification and MA state license, or the ability to obtain before starting
  • CPR/AED certification, or willingness to obtain upon hiring
  • 1-3 years of experience, ideally in an independent school setting or with adolescents in an educational setting
  • Strong organizational and collaborative skills
  • Excellent interpersonal skills and the ability to work with a wide array of constituents
  • (students, coaches, parents, faculty, health care providers)
  • Working knowledge of Microsoft Office, Google Workspace, and related record-keeping software (SportsWare)
  • Inclusive approach to teaching and educating
  • Solid communications skills
  • A positive attitude and commitment to teamwork and collaboration

Compensation & Benefits:
This is a 10-month per year position coinciding with the academic year (late August to early June) with a starting salary range of $50,000 - $55,000 commensurate with experience.

This is a benefits-eligible position, with access to all benefits available to full-time staff members, including, but not limited to: health, dental, and vision insurance, 403(b) retirement savings contributions, and paid time off.

Interested candidates should send a resume and cover letter to jobs@groton.org.


Groton School is an equal opportunity employer.
 

Part-Time School Store and Mailroom Associate

Groton School is seeking a part-time School Store & Mailroom Associate to assist in the operation of the school's Store and Mailroom. A customer service-oriented team player, the Associate's duties include working the register in the store, sorting and distributing mail and packages, and handling student petty cash transactions.

This position reports to the School Store Manager and is for approximately 16 hours per week from September through June, with reduced hours in July and August. Additional hours may be required as needed for special events. 

Schedule

From September to June while school is in session (i.e., excluding student breaks):

Mondays: 9:30 a.m.–3:00 p.m. in the Mailroom
Wednesdays: 9:30 a.m.–1:00 p.m. in the Mailroom
Fridays: 8:30 a.m.–3:00 p.m. in the School Store
Two Saturdays Per Month: 9:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m. (in Mailroom or Store depending on need)
Additional hours as needed during first two weeks of school, Parents Weekend, Reunion Weekend, and Prize Day

Qualifications

Qualified candidates will have:

  • A high school diploma or equivalent
  • Customer service experience and a positive attitude
  • The ability to work in a fast-paced environment with high school students and adults
  • Strong organizational and communication skills
  • The ability to process and accurately record simple financial transactions
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office (Excel and Word) and Google Apps

This is a non-exempt, non-benefited position with an hourly pay rate of $21.00.

Interested candidates should send a resume and cover letter to: jobs@groton.org with School Store & Mailroom Associate in the subject line.

Groton School is an equal-opportunity employer.

Become Part of Groton's Vision


A coeducational, primarily residential school of 383 students, Groton sets for its students the highest standards of academic achievement, intellectual growth, ethical awareness and behavior, sportsmanship, athletic endeavor, and service to others.

The school takes leadership seriously, and our concern for character is a point of distinction. Our mission goes well beyond preparing students for college, instead aiming to provide an experience that addresses students' growth and maturity. With a balance of tradition and innovation, Groton provides exceptional opportunities to deserving students from diverse social, geographic, ethnic, and socioeconomic backgrounds, and accepts students without regard to their families' ability to pay tuition.

If you are applying to become a member of Groton’s faculty, you should understand that Groton believes it is essential that teachers take part in students' lives. Groton's faculty does what the best faculties do: it builds student-teacher relationships that reach beyond the classroom and affirm and nurture the spirit while challenging the mind. Faculty members are expected to fulfill the founding vision that they take up the teaching profession as "their life's work."

An inclusive, tight-knit community, the Groton faculty is dedicated to inspiring student lives of character, learning, leadership, and service.
