Groton Fund

The Groton Fund supports every aspect of life at Groton, including faculty compensation, financial aid, arts, athletics, student life, diversity and inclusion, and maintenance of our campus.

CLICK HERE TO MAKE YOUR GIFT

Each year parents, alumni, grandparents, faculty, and friends help the Groton Fund reach its goal, bolstering the school’s fiscal strength. These gifts help preserve the endowment, ensuring that Groton continues to provide an exceptional education for both current students and generations to come.

The Groton Fund contributes about 12 percent of Groton’s annual budget, resulting in many of the school’s extraordinary opportunities for students.

An essential part of the Groton Fund is the Parent Fund, which—thanks to our dedicated and generous parents—also provides critical support to the school.
