Childcare

Little Zebras (for children ages 5+): 
We offer a "Little Zebras" kids program for children ages 5+. On Saturday from 4:30 p.m. to 9:30 p.m. children will have the opportunity to play games and sports in the Groton School Athletic Center,  enjoy a pizza dinner, do crafts, and watch a movie. There will be a fee per child. Please add this option when you register for the reunion. 

You must also complete the "Little Zebras" registration form. Click here to download.  Please email this form along with a photo of your child(ren) to canderson@groton.org.


For Alumni bringing children under the age of 5, please see the below.

Babysitting Service: For families looking for individual daycare or care for children under the age of 5, please visit Summersitters.com for local babysitters. Families can also download the Summer Sitters App directly from the App store and submit requests there.

Please reach out to Charlotte Anderson at canderson@groton.org with any specific questions or for assistance.

Athletic Hall of Fame

The Groton School Athletic Hall of Fame honors past student athletes, faculty, staff, and friends who have brought distinction through athletics to Groton School and themselves. Consideration will also be given to athletes who display a lasting commitment to the mission of Groton School.

See Hall of Fame Inductees

 

Alumni Awards

Since 1977, Groton School has presented the Distinguished Grotonian Award, which recognizes graduates whose lives of highly distinguished service reflect the essential values of the School. The Cui Servire Award is given annually to outstanding younger alumni who, through their exceptional contribution to the School or the world, have truly lived up to the School's motto.

See Alumni Awards
