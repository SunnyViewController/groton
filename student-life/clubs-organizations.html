Clubs & Organizations

Want to sing with friends . . . or play chess . . . or debate? Practice yoga or learn ukulele? You name it—if you’ve got an interest, we’ve got a club. And if we don’t have the club you want, you can easily start it.

Some of the clubs and organizations started and led by our students:

  • Acolytes
  • Astronomy Club
  • Bell Ringing
  • Business & Management Club
  • Christian Fellowship
  • Circle Voice
  • Classics Club
  • Color Guard
  • Computer Science Club
  • Cultural Alliance
  • Current Events Club
  • Debating Society
  • Digital Marketing

Acolytes

We acolyte during Sunday chapel. Minor strength required.

Astronomy Club

Astronomy club is a place where students who are interested in any kind of astronomy are welcome to ask and learn more about space in general. This is a relaxing, informative club where students will come by to either delve deeper into astronomy or simply relax while watching the stars. 

Bell Ringing

We're a club that would like to introduce the unique opportunity to ring the chapel bells.  It is a fun and intricate activity that not many people have the opportunity to do. 

Business & Management Club

Educate members about business practice, find and interview business people to speak to the club about their experiences and give leadership advice.

Christian Fellowship

Spread the gospel, pray, read the Bible, bound together. Always a super fun and informative time!

Circle Voice

The Circle Voice is Groton's student-run school newspaper. We are looking for all kinds of passionate individuals who will help us combine videography, photography, podcasts, and journalism to enrich our publication. 

Classics Club

We are focused on scholarship of Latin and Greek; we offer peer tutoring as well as regular meetings about literature, history, and culture of the ancient world.

Color Guard

We raise and lower the flag every evening and morning. Low time commitment, honorable task at hand.

Computer Science Club

CS@Groton is open to both experienced programmers and complete beginners. The club's activities will range from teaching programming concepts to preparing for CS contests to building programming projects.

Cultural Alliance

Originally, the Alliance was named Black Awareness Society, and it was created in the early ’70s as an affinity group for Black students. Sometime in the ’80s, the name was changed to the Cultural Alliance for Student Harmony, and in the past few decades, it has changed from an affinity group to an initiative for diversity. Today, our purpose is to provide a space for students to discuss and educate each other on not only racial injustices but also other societal issues, on campus and around the world. Our biggest responsibilities as a club are running MLK Day and hosting meetings.

Current Events Club

The Current Events Club is a discussion-based club where all students with various opinions can talk about prevalent issues around the world. This club sparks unique conversations and allows students to share their perspectives and ideas about global issues in an open and inclusive setting. Previously, we have discussed issues such as the Israeli–Palestinian conflict, school shootings and gun violence in the U.S., and the Russia–Ukraine War.

Debating Society

The Debating Society is Groton’s premier student organization dedicated to debate and public speaking. Throughout the year, we compete in tournaments with independent schools across New England, both at the novice and advanced levels, usually in parliamentary-style debates. The Society has enjoyed immense interscholastic success in recent years and members are invited to improve their skills by participating in regular practices. In addition, the Society hosts a variety of events on campus to promote debate and public speaking, such as the long-standing tradition of sit-down debates which engage students and faculty alike in fun, engaging discourse. 

Digital Marketing

Our club focuses on combining creativity and marketing to make a real-world impact. This year, we’ll be working alongside nonprofits and small businesses in need of support in areas such as:

  • Posters and graphic design advertisements
  • Social media management
  • Raising awareness/ attracting customers

If you’re interested in entering the business/marketing world in the future or are interested in photography, graphic design, or videography, this is the perfect opportunity to use your interests and skills for meaningful causes.

Envirothon

Envirothon is an environmental science competition focused on hands-on, team-based problem solving. This club prepares for the annual state competition in May, while also learning about Groton's ecosystems. In past years, we've won accolades at both the state and national level.

Essence Step Team

Groton School’s official step team. We try to introduce students to a style of dance that includes using clapping and stomping to make synchronized sounds.  

French Club

Our goal is to embrace the French language and culture at Groton further than just what is provided in French classes. We do so by sharing French food, presentations on holidays and traditions (& culture), current news, homework help, and always providing a space is speak French. 

Geography Group

Groton Geography Group meets weekly to discuss the history, culture, geography, political landscape, and any other aspect of a country. Each meeting, we talk about the physical geography, history, economy, and culture of one country and play a kahoot! The winner of each kahoot receives the honor of picking next week’s country. 

Grotonian

A student-run literature and arts magazine

Girls Who Code

A club that provides a comfortable space for young students to learn about the world of code and get help with coding projects. 

Groton Feminists

At Groton Feminists we plan to have a variety of conversations beyond the classic gendered issues, discussing things from the gender pay gap to the importance of diversity in medicine. We hold open meetings throughout the year to discuss feminist issues on campus and throughout the world, so always feel free to stop by! 

Gender Sexuality Alliance (GSA)

GSA is a safe and open space for Groton students to discuss LGBTQ+ issues that exist within the school and the greater world. We organize informational meetings and discussions about Groton's queer community. We also communicate with the administration/trustees to improve the queer experience on campus.

Hang Up & Hang Out

We began this as an challenge, encouraging our friends to give up their phones. We hope to make this opportunity more accessible to students, aiding them in transferring from smart phones to flip phones so that they can engage more in the present. We promise that it will be life changing and refreshing!

High School Law Review

The Groton High School Law Review will discuss cases, learn how to write legal briefs and teach students about the law and Supreme Court. 

Hiking/Outdoors Club

Our goal is to provide opportunities for students to be able to go on outdoor trips in the neighboring area and to provide the community with an outlet to the outdoors. 

History Club

We will be having club discussions on history topics. We also have two optional projects (National Archive and National History Day) that we will be working on together. We will try our best to create a comfortable space for everyone who are interested in history to meet each other and learn more about our world. 

Investment Club

We discuss the technicalities and social implications of the economy and the stock market, invite guest speakers (including Groton alums) in finance, and manage a real portfolio of that the school provides us. We host biweekly meetings to discuss our holdings and trades.

Lit and Poetry Society

This is a club where literature-lovers can come together to discuss poetry and short stories. Meetings are casual gatherings every two weeks where we talk about a piece of writing. Every meeting has different readings that range from poetry and short stories to excerpts of longer works. We also urge members to share their own work with the group. We do writing workshops, and even perform slam poetry. 

Maqupellas

Maqupellas is a co-ed acapella group. We have practices once a week after school to prepare for performances such as the parents weekend, Groton Pops, and the Spring Instrumental Concert.

Math Club

A club open to all of campus for students who like math. We host open meetings and math competitions for students to participate in.

Mixed Heritage Alliance (MHA)

MHA provides a safe and inclusive space for multiracial and multiethnic students to connect, share their stories, and explore what it means to have a mixed identity. We foster a sense of belonging and community, embracing the unique experiences that come with navigating multiple cultures and backgrounds. The club also serves as an educational resource for the wider school community, organizing events to promote cultural awareness and empathy. 

Model United Nations

At Model UN/Model Congress, we research, discuss, and debate about social and political issues in the national and global scale. They are each simulations of the United Nations and the United States Congress, respectively.

Our main event is our winter term trip to either Harvard Model UN or Harvard Model Congress conference. We also hold in-house conferences. It's a great activity to participate in if you're passionate about current events!

Philosophy Club

We all have a shared interest in philosophical questions of many varieties. We hope to provide a space where students can take a moment for introspection as we ponder things we don’t normally think or discuss about. Topics will include: paradoxes, logic, moral questioning, societal evaluation+remedy, existentialist questions.

Photography Club

The club will try to meet once a month, discuss photography techniques and famous photographers, and also provide help for any interested new photographers. 

Psychology Club

Our club aims to help introduce students to the study of psychology through discussions and interactive activities such as movies, debates, and games. This club is open to people with or without previous psychology knowledge.

Robotics Club

Our club focuses on building, programing, and driving a Vex V5 Robot for the VRC (Vex Robotics Competition). The club has competed at the regional level, winning an Innovate Award in March 2023. We run a recreational group to teach the basics of robotics and a competitive team for VRC events.

Rocket Club

We teach members to build model rockets and compete in national competitions. Returning and new members work towards TARC, a national rocketry competition. Our goal is to participate in TARC and strengthen our rocket-building skills. All levels of experience are welcome!

Sign Language Club

A club to teach Groton students basic signs from SEE (Signing Exact English).  We would meet once a month for an hour Zoom call with Alise George, a SEE Class Coordinator from the Northwest School for Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing Children, who would teach intro signs and then meet another time that month to practice together. Learning SEE sign language is important because it will allow Groton students to communicate with more people; with people who are hard of hearing and those who are deaf. We would also learn more about sign language and its importance. 

Ski Club

During winter term, we organize some buses to go to Wachusett Mountain a few times. It is fine if you have never skied or snowboarded before. Some equipment rentals and tickets may be subsidized by the school.

Sustainability Committee

The Sustainability Committee is the main student run group that works on various sustainable initiatives on campus. We work with faculty to drive Groton's sustainability efforts, from recycling to donation drives we're always looking for new ideas and driven students to make a difference.  

Tabletop Gaming

TCG meets once a week to play board games and card games. Stop by for a fun time! 

Young Democrats

Young Democrats is a club dedicated to the values of the Democratic Party that strives to educate students about political issues. We also promote bipartisan discussions and work to create spaces for students to explore their budding political thought. 

Young Republicans

Young Republicans is an on-campus political group that encourages political, intellectual discussions about philosophy while staying open-minded. In collaboration with the Young Democrats, we also host meetings about current affairs and bring in outside speakers.
