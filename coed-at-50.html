Welcome and Groton’s Coeducation Journey

Open the afternoon with a review of Groton’s process leading to coeducation and following the transition, with first-hand accounts from faculty and alumni.

Moderator: Richard Bradley '82. Panelists: David Bannard P'01, '03, Past Faculty (1972-1989), Pam Clarke, Past Faculty (1972-1990), Alyce Jones Lee '77, Bill Polk ’58, Headmaster, LuAnn Polk, Naomi Pollock ’77, and Ben Pyne ’77, P'12, '15, Trustee.

Generations of Coeducation

Hear stories and perspectives of the opportunities afforded by coeducation. Alumni family members across generations will share their Groton experiences, with a special focus on the school’s transition from a single-sex institution to a coeducational community. 

Moderator: Adam Spence '92, P'22,'25,'27, Trustee. Panelists: Jim Cooper '72, Mary Cooper '08, Trustee, Bill Cross '77, Minturn Osborne '79, Polly Cross Reeve '78, Former Trustee, and Lucretia "Lukie" Osborne Wells '81.

Evolution of Groton’s Curriculum

Learn about the evolution of Groton’s curriculum with a focus on coeducation and inclusion. 

Moderator: Laurie Sales, Current Faculty. Panelists: Mary Frances Bannard P'29, Current Faculty, Jonathan Choate '60, P'85,'88,'88, Past Faculty (1969-2018), Kathy Leggat, Current Faculty, Amy Martin Nelson, Current Faculty, and Sravani Sen-Das P'16,'19,'23, Current Faculty. 

Groton Leadership and Advocacy

An exploration of leadership at Groton: learn how Groton shapes leaders in many ways, including the adoption of a co-prefect system and examples of advocacy. 

Moderator: Lori Diane Hill ’88, Former Trustee. Panelists: John Dowling '90, Marianne Lu '19, Alex Manugian '90, Layla McDermott ’18, and Kate Milliken ’90.

  • A Coeducational Reflection on Groton’s Arts Program
  • Groton Women’s Network—Grotonians in Medicine and STEM
  • Groton Athletic Competitions

A Coeducational Reflection on Groton’s Arts Program

From the early days, when boys played female roles in theater productions (as was typical at the time), to the welcoming of girls into the (previously all-male) choir and beyond, explore how the introduction of coeducation has shaped the arts at Groton today.  

Moderator: James Bundy '77, P'09, '12, Former Trustee. Panelists: John Donovan '20, Kristen Donovan, Current Faculty, Daniel Hathaway, Past Faculty (1974-1977), and Laurie Sales, Current Faculty.
 

Groton Women’s Network—Grotonians in Medicine and STEM

A panel discussion featuring professionals working across the fields of STEM and medicine to share their diverse paths, experiences, and insights. 

Moderator: Carolyn Chica, Current Faculty. Panelists: Amy Baughman '99, Autumn Johnson '19, Ade Osinubi ’14, and Mimi Sotiriou Raygorodetsky ’94. 

Groton Athletic Competitions

Watch boys and girls soccer, field hockey, and varsity volleyball compete with St. George's. Football will take on Portsmouth Abbey. View the Athletics Calendar.

Groton Women’s Network—What’s Your Story? Telling It Across Film, News, Social Media, and Books

An interactive session about discovering your voice and crafting compelling stories—no matter the medium. 

Moderator and Panelist: Kate Milliken ’90. Panelists: Heather Clark ’92, P'26, Ade Osinubi ’14, Karla Marie Sanford ’19, and Becky Zhang ’18.
 

Athletics at Groton

A panel discussion highlighting and celebrating Groton athletes, current and past. 

Moderator: Preston Bannard '01, P'29, Current Faculty. Panelists: Jaden Adinkrah '23, David Bannard P'01,'03, Past Faculty (1972-1989), Claudia Asano Barcomb ’95, Amy Frothingham Ford '78, Vuyelwa Maqubela, Current Faculty, Jennifer Polynice ’25, Hope Nichols Prockop ’86, P'15, '17.

Stories from the Fall of 1975

Hear first-hand accounts and memories from the pioneers of 1975—those who experienced Groton’s transition to coeducation—as they share personal stories that bring this pivotal moment in the School’s history to life.

Moderator: Naomi Pollock '77. Panelists: Diana Chigas '79, Former Trustee, Ellie Dwight '77, Alyce Jones Lee '77, Laura Rogerson Moore ’78, and Polly Cross Reeve '78, Former Trustee.

Evolution of a Logo

During the 24-25 winter term, art student's in Ms. Ho's graphic design class where given a final project for the history books: designing a logo to represent the 50th anniversary of coeducation at Groton School. All four students in the class created designs which represented what
coeducation meant to them. Daniel Velazqez '25's logo was selected. The logo design was then refined by a professional design firm, and a visual identity was developed by John Donovan '20.

History

1884

Endicott Peabody founded Groton School.

1885

Fannie Peabody married Headmaster Endicott Peabody and joined him on campus.

1932

Groton's first female teacher, Betsey Peabody, began teaching science and reading.

1940

The Peabodys retire after 56 years. John Crocker is named Groton School's second Headmaster, joined by his wide, Mary Crocker. 

1950

First chair of the UN Commission on Human Rights Eleanor Roosevelt spoke at Groton.

1957

Headmaster John Crocker and the first Coeducation Committee considered founding a sister school on the old golf course.

1965

Bertrand Honea is named Groton's third headmaster, joined by his wife, Patrica Honea. Headmaster Honea contemplated a merger with an existing all-girls school. 

1965

Groton hired its first cohort of female faculty members.

1969

Paul Wright is named Groton School's fourth Headmaster, joined by his wife, Margaret Wright. 

1970

Headmaster Paul Wright and the Board of Trustees convene the Commitee on Coeducation to consider admitting girls. 

1971

The Board of Trustees approved the Committee's recommendation that Groton go coed.

1972

The Board of Trustees unanimously reaffirmed its approval of the Committee's recommendation amid alumni pushback.

1973

Rowland Cox was named Headmaster and oversaw the transition to coeducation along with his wife Mary Cox.

1973

The Board of Trustees approved fundraising, renovation, and building projects to prepare for the arrival of female students.

1974

A final Coeducation Committee was formed to address new policies related to curricula, school rules, and athletics.

1974

Anne Tottenham became the first female dean.

1974

Renovations in Hundred House are completed. The former servant quarters are transformed into girls' dormitory units 4 and 6.

1975

The first female students matriculated at Groton in September 1975.

1977

The first coed form graduated.

1977

Peter Camp, joined by his wife, Patricia Camp, fills in as interim headmaster for the 1977–1978 school year.

1978

Bill Polk is named Groton School's sixth headmaster, joined by his wife LuAnn Polk. 

1980

Inter-visitation was granted experimentally, becoming fully codified the next school year. 

1990

Faculty successfully advocated for two senior prefects: one male, one female. 

2003

Richard Commons is named Groton's seventh headmaster, joined by his wife Lindsay Commons.

2004

The ratio of female and male students reached equilibrium.

2013

Temba Maqubela is named Groton's School's eighth headmaster, joined by his wife, Vuyelwa Maqubela.

2014

Headmaster Temba Maqubela introduces an affordability and inclusion initiative known as GRAIN that expands access to all students regardless of their financial need. 

2018–2022

Advocacy by a group of female students was successful in adding diversity to Groton's bust collection in the Schoolroom. The new busts include Rosa Parks, Eleanor Roosevelt, Mahatma Gandhi, and Nelson Mandela.  

2023

Anne Mosle '83 received the Distinguished Grotonian award, the first time it has been bestowed on a woman. 

2025

In April 2025, the GRAIN Initiative reached $100 million raised for financial aid and tuition containment. 

2025

Groton School celebrates its 50th anniversary of coeducation!
